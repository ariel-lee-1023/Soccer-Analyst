## Corrections, synthesis and tensions

- **Beggs Poisson notation:** the supplied rendering around equations 6.1–6.2 conflates response with conditional mean and displays a sum of exponentials. Runtime uses `log(lambda)=eta`, `lambda=exp(eta)`. Zero observed goals remain possible.
- **Beggs graph density:** equations 8.1–8.2 are labelled as maximum edges but show density ratios. Runtime distinguishes possible edges `k*(k−1)` from observed-edge density `L/[k*(k−1)]`; path weights and disconnected graphs receive advisor qualifications.
- **Beggs rankings:** the Elo introduction broadly contrasts Colley/Massey as not considering who plays whom, but the preceding matrix definitions include opponent counts. Runtime retains schedule adjustment and isolates Elo's sequential updating as the distinction.
- **McElreath mathematical conversion:** minus signs and symbols are missing in several rendered formulas. Posterior conjugacy and standard WAIC sign were reconstructed from definitions; no raw formula dump was used.
- **Graham usage:** source verification tightened the definition to possession-ending actions, added the triple threat and opposition adjustment, and separated attribution leakage from future-data leakage.
- **Statistical methods:** Beggs uses classical tests but resists rigid significance gates; McElreath develops inference without those tests. The skill retains methods with their assumptions rather than inventing one unified school.
- **Tactics vs economics:** Wilson/Cox retain spatial mechanisms and historical context. Kuper/Szymanski retain resource and market hypotheses. The new core prevents either long-run resources or descriptive historical patterns from substituting for match-specific causal evidence. The old references were not rewritten or rescanned as new distillations.
- **Synthesis:** sampling denominators, the compact evidence schema, reproducibility record, football model applications and the evidence→comparison→uncertainty→role→commitment sequence are library contributions. They are not attributed to one author.
