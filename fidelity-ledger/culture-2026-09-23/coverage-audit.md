# Selective coverage and fidelity audit

The purpose is elite-football leadership, team psychology and organizational culture. This is a selective fold-in, not an exhaustive substitute for six books. The inventory below records the task-relevant structures identified through contents, heading/keyword probes and source slices. It was reconciled after drafting, not falsely described as an independently frozen exhaustive framework inventory. Locators are book chapters/section names plus local lines in the per-source extracted body, counted from 1; source file hashes are in the manifest. Markdown conversion page estimates are not print pagination.

## Nesti — organizational foundation

**Retained:** person-centred/existential grounding; the distinction between mental-skills work and organizational practice; staff role descriptions, appraisal, CPD, buy-in, codes of conduct and away days; manager reflection; human spirit and the limitations of its empirical evaluation; team communication; banter; liaison support; confidentiality, identity, equality and living values. Eight chapter-level sections preserve the source spine. The detailed intervention table is explicitly library synthesis.

**Compressed:** practical recruitment and employment advice, staff mentorship, pastoral provision, and the final chapter's football lessons. **Excluded:** full counselling dialogues, detailed philosophical/theological exposition, remuneration advice and the full “Big Ten” list. They are not needed for this task and would risk presenting an analyst as a counselling practitioner. Nesti's semi-fictitious manager dialogue is explicitly labelled, not treated as an observed case.

**Main locators:** chapter 3, lines 640–728 and 766–792; chapter 5, 1280–1322; chapter 7, 1644–1660 and 1776–1842. **Additional audit samples outside the initial main framework slice:** 1480–1496 (“When the banter stops”) adds coercion/bullying and silence as self-protection; 1508–1528 (liaison officer) adds family/settlement and different access relationships. Both retained. Verification at 382–410 confirms confidentiality's tension with interdisciplinary information sharing, and 476–488 prevents falsely recasting Nesti as exclusively measurement-oriented.

**Boundary:** “main foundation” describes this library's routing, not a claim that the book is the strongest contemporary systematic evidence. Its historical statements about research gaps are not asserted as the current state of psychology.

## Beswick — collective methods

**Retained:** Direction / Relationships / Performance; all fifteen chapter headings grouped under those parts; Unifying Purpose, Creating Positivity, Focusing Challenge, Encouraging Leaders, Forming Bonds, Fostering Coachability, Requiring Accountability, Synching Aptitudes, Managing Mood, Competing Cohesively, Summoning Momentum, Handling Pressure, Overcoming Adversity, Keeping Fresh and Repeating Success. Preserve all named components of the Ten Key Elements of the Accountability Cycle and Seven Steps to Team Cohesion; task/social cohesion; four coachability levels; five energy domains; player leadership groups. Chapter-level summaries do not claim every internal technique was extracted.

**Compressed:** momentum and adversity material into limited application boundaries; role-teaching examples; the mood chapter into environment/relationship effects. **Excluded:** complete worksheets, scoring checklists, exercise variants, reproduced tables/figures and coaching maxims not necessary for the user's remit. The uninspected detail of the eight-item Focusing Challenge list is not reconstructed. Sex-based generalizations in the bonding/criticism discussion are not imported as diagnostic rules for individual players.

**Main locators:** 2985–3040, 3140–3295; 3605–3638; 4316–4480; 6056–6257; 6384–6433; 8700–8790. **Additional audit samples:** 3146–3151 warns against assuming the reason for withdrawal (retained); 6241–6257 explicitly allows professional task cohesion with less social cohesion and notes that happiness and success diverge (retained). 9030–9065 adds chance and renewed appraisal after success; 1010–1070 shows that the book's positivity anecdotes make stronger causal claims than the library should repeat. Retain the practical interpretation, reject an inferred efficacy estimate.

**Disagreement preserved:** “completely coachable” includes surrender of voice. The reference names this, then distinguishes it from the library's insistence on reasoned dissent and Ancelotti's two-way learning. The anonymous-question example is from a university women's team; no elite-team trial is claimed. Text extraction damaged tables, so no uncertain numerical/scoring layout was reproduced.

## Ancelotti — relational authority

**Retained:** leadership arc; The Family, International Culture, Cultural Bridge; Managing Up; talent and onboarding; personality/technical/political leaders; captaincy versus influence; explicit/implicit rules and culture/climate; negotiated means and enforced commitments; engagement/alignment; listening; anger over behaviour rather than football error; reflection and switching off. Shared meals/seating, Drogba's lateness and the GPS dispute retain specific mechanisms and attribution.

**Compressed:** all ten chapters are represented, with The Product/Data and Growing/Values treated as contextual support. **Excluded:** full career chronology, testimonials, claimed managerial cycle length as a universal law, national-character stereotypes, and detailed tactical prescriptions. Later coaching outcomes cannot be supplied by this 2016 edition.

**Main locators:** 297–329; 549–599; 639–699. **Additional audit samples:** 401–411 (Managing Up) adds ambiguous reporting after the loss of intermediaries; 519–527 (Talent) adds the difficulty of entering a strong culture and the risk of demotivation. 341–351 verification makes the “cultural bridge” concrete: staff linking country, institution and newcomers. The GPS episode at 591 contains differing player interpretations of the same monitoring practice. Retained without treating frequency as sports-science guidance.

**Quality:** the supplied Markdown systematically drops letters, including from some names and headings. Obvious titles and personal names are normalized; no fabricated verbatim quotations or repair of the entire book. Unclear text was not used to make precise numerical claims. There was no substitute original-language edition against which to verify every OCR repair.

## O'Brien edited volume — optional interpretation

**Retained:** symbol, projection, complexes, individuation/ego–Self, Persona, Shadow, Anima/Animus (strongly bounded); The Transcendent Function; stage/relational field and amplification; Beswick's reciprocal team contract and “soul”; selected player-first concerns; mastery/performance climate distinction; appendix design and the explicit difference between trait and Jungian type theory. Contributor identities remain attached to chapters.

**Compressed:** historical/philosophical chapters 1 and 3–5 and the context of chapters 7–8. **Excluded:** diagnostic/therapeutic instructions, inferred individual trauma, metaphysical assertions as facts, full historical argument, UBRING COPRACTICS coach typology, and the uninspected remainder of Seven Ways of Putting the Player First. The latter is explicitly partial in the reference, not advertised as the full named method. No purported psychometric selection instrument is created.

**Main locators:** 447–471, 647–693, 1571–1595, 1999–2021, 2130–2195. **Additional audit samples:** 2670–2704 preserves the qualification that mastery climates do not eliminate competition and separates observation of aggression from a speculative trauma cause; 2888–2916 establishes the youth-camp setting, observer design and BFI/type boundary. Both materially limit transfer to elite teams. The reported basketball dinner bargain at 2138 exposes status privileges as well as relationship-building; it is retained as an example with that risk, not a recommended selection guarantee.

**Evidence dependency:** Beswick here and in One Goal is overlapping practitioner provenance. This chapter cites Honigstein's Klopp book. Counting three retellings as independent evidence would be wrong.

**Main locators:** 2730–2830, 2870–2940, 6810–6860, 9015–9065, 9100–9128. **Additional audit samples:** 6635–6652 shows reported cohesion through relegation; 9770–9825 gives recruitment, roles, disrupted automatisms and finance as alternatives to lost charisma. **Quality:** front-cover OCR is severely damaged; selected body text is usable. Physical PDF page count is distinguished from printed-page locators. No clean cover-text reconstruction is claimed.

**Compressed:** nineteen narrative chapters grouped by organizational question. **Excluded:** full campaign recaps, detailed player biographies, all historical financial statistics, and the complete tactical diffusion history. No claim that a camp or reform alone determined a knockout outcome.

**Main locators:** 237–245, 501–517, 611–617, 1092–1116. **Additional audit samples:** 763–767 adds selection, role availability and prior club form as alternatives to camp design; 953–961 and 976 show an unplanned social use of analysis technology and the difference between planned tracking and actual video functions. The barefoot table-tennis episode adds a concrete cost to an otherwise celebratory narrative.

## Cross-source boundary and cost review

The core's intervention sequence, status-sensitive observation plan and keep/adapt/end criteria are original synthesis aligned with the user's stated purpose. No author is credited with that complete framework. Nesti/Beswick supply practice methods, Ancelotti practitioner authority, Honigstein situated reporting and the edited volume an optional symbolic lens. None alone supplies a modern systematic review or an identified performance effect for a meal, dress or hotel policy.

All new references are selective, approximately 1.9–2.8k script-density tokens, well below their study/text budgets and the stricter 14k cap. They are not padded toward targets. The metatool's 50–70-token named-item calibration is not treated as a validated quality metric for case-heavy biographies and an edited collection. Actual token measurements are in validation.json.

Some initial slice calls exceeded output display budgets, especially long Markdown paragraphs. Requested ranges are recorded separately from confirmed-complete reads; conservative coverage does not credit their full spans. This prevents an exact reading-cost or exhaustive-coverage claim. Later verification used clean_slice; no output savings occurred for those already mostly prose spans. No whole-corpus read was used.
